### Scrum Facilitator — Goal

#### Outcome

Become the tool a Scrum Master opens every sprint without thinking about it.

#### Why this app can win it

It has the highest natural repeat frequency in the suite — every ceremony,
every sprint, forever. Habit is the moat, and habit is won by being faster to
open than the alternative and by remembering what happened last time.

#### Signals

1. Consecutive sprints a team runs through it.
2. Participants per session — facilitation pulls whole teams in.
3. Retro notes and actions carried from one sprint into the next.
4. GitHub stars and mentions in Scrum Master communities.

#### Boundaries

- Not a Jira replacement, not a task tracker.
- Not prescriptive about a team's flavour of Scrum.
- Ceremonies stay runnable by a facilitator who has never read the docs.
